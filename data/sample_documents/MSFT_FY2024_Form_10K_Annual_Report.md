### ITEM 1. BUSINESS

#### Strategic Overview & Generative AI Transformation
Microsoft is a technology company whose mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. In fiscal year 2024, Microsoft accelerated its transition to an AI-first platform company, embedding generative AI capabilities across our entire tech stack through Microsoft Copilot and our strategic partnership with OpenAI.

We operate three reportable segments:
1. **Productivity and Business Processes:** Includes Office Commercial (Microsoft 365 Commercial products and cloud services), Office Consumer, LinkedIn, and Dynamics business solutions (Dynamics 365).
2. **Intelligent Cloud:** Consists of server products and cloud services, including Azure and other cloud services, Windows Server, SQL Server, Visual Studio, System Center, and GitHub.
3. **More Personal Computing:** Includes Windows OEM and Commercial licensing, Devices (Surface), Gaming (Xbox content, consoles, and Activision Blizzard post-acquisition), and Search and news advertising.

#### Fiscal Year 2024 Financial Highlights
- **Total Revenue:** $245.1 billion, an increase of $33.2 billion or 16% compared to $211.9 billion in fiscal year 2023.
- **Operating Income:** $109.4 billion, an increase of $20.9 billion or 24% compared to $88.5 billion in fiscal year 2023.
- **Operating Margin:** 44.6%, expanding by approximately 290 basis points compared to 41.8% in fiscal year 2023.
- **Net Income:** $88.1 billion, an increase of 22% compared to $72.4 billion in fiscal year 2023.
- **Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS):** $11.80, up 22% compared to $9.68 in fiscal year 2023.
- **Capital Expenditures (including finance leases):** $55.7 billion, driven by cloud and AI infrastructure investments (GPUs, data centers, network architecture).

### ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

#### 1. AI Safety, Alignment, and Emerging Global Regulatory Oversight
Our strategic positioning depends on the successful deployment of AI technologies. As AI capabilities evolve rapidly, they present operational, regulatory, and reputational risks:
- New legal frameworks such as the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) impose rigorous transparency, copyright compliance, and conformity assessment obligations.
- Algorithmic bias, flawed outputs ("hallucinations"), or inadvertent disclosure of proprietary customer data could result in regulatory penalties, litigation, and brand impairment.
- Increased competition from specialized hyperscalers and open-source foundation models may exert downward pricing pressure on AI inference services.

#### 2. Cloud Infrastructure & Specialized Hardware Supply Constraints
Our Intelligent Cloud segment and Azure AI platform rely on specialized accelerator hardware (GPUs and custom ASICs). Global semiconductor supply bottlenecks, export control regulations (such as US restrictions on advanced computing chips to certain foreign markets), or datacenter power interconnection delays could constrain our capacity to meet surging customer demand.

#### 3. Cybersecurity Vulnerabilities & Nation-State Threats
As a global hyperscaler, Microsoft is subject to sophisticated cyberattacks by nation-state actors and organized adversaries targeting identity infrastructure (such as Microsoft Entra ID) and customer tenant data. A major breach could result in severe commercial damages, contractual liabilities, and regulatory enforcement.

### ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS (MD&A)

#### Segment Performance Analysis

##### 1. Intelligent Cloud Segment
- **Revenue:** $105.4 billion in FY2024, compared to $87.9 billion in FY2023, representing growth of 20% (20% in constant currency).
- **Azure and Other Cloud Services:** Revenue grew 30% driven by broad-based enterprise migrations and contributions from Azure AI services. Azure AI customer base exceeded 60,000 enterprise organizations.
- **Operating Income:** $49.6 billion, up 28% compared to $38.8 billion in FY2023. Operating margin expanded to 47.1%.

##### 2. Productivity and Business Processes Segment
- **Revenue:** $77.7 billion in FY2024, compared to $69.3 billion in FY2023, growth of 12% (12% in constant currency).
- **Office Commercial Products & Cloud Services:** Grew 13%, driven by Office 365 Commercial seat growth of 11% and increasing Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) following Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts.
- **LinkedIn:** Grew 10%, driven by Talent Solutions and Marketing Solutions.
- **Dynamics 365:** Grew 22%, reflecting strong momentum in enterprise ERP and CRM cloud workloads.
- **Operating Income:** $40.3 billion, up 18% compared to $34.2 billion in FY2023.

##### 3. More Personal Computing Segment
- **Revenue:** $62.0 billion in FY2024, compared to $54.7 billion in FY2023, growth of 13% (13% in constant currency), primarily reflecting the consolidation of Activision Blizzard.
- **Windows OEM:** Grew 8%, driven by commercial PC demand and Windows 11 enterprise migration.
- **Gaming Revenue:** Grew 39%, driven by Activision Blizzard acquisition net of integration expenses.
- **Operating Income:** $19.5 billion, up 26% compared to $15.5 billion in FY2023.

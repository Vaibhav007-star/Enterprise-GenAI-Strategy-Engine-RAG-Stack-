# Enterprise Data Governance & Retention Policy

**Document Reference:** POL-GOV-2024-v3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Enterprise Policy  
**Review Cycle:** Annual (Next Scheduled: November 2024)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This policy governs the retention, classification, privacy handling, and legal compliance obligations for all structured and unstructured information assets generated across enterprise business units. Compliance is mandatory for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors.

## 2. Information Classification Framework

The enterprise categorizes data assets into four discrete confidentiality tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Public:** Information approved for external dissemination (marketing collateral, public press releases).
- **Tier 2 — Internal Business:** General corporate records, non-sensitive emails, operational workflow documentation.
- **Tier 3 — Confidential:** Customer records, financial performance forecasts, pricing models, employee performance metrics.
- **Tier 4 — Restricted / PII:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII), biometric data, payment card numbers, and core intellectual property. Access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) and explicit role authorization.

## 3. Mandatory Retention Schedules & Deadlines

All business units must enforce the following document retention schedules:

1. **Financial and Accounting Records:** General ledgers, tax filings, and audited annual balance sheets must be retained for exactly **7 years** from the date of filing.
2. **Employee Records:** Personnel files, compensation history, and benefits enrollment data must be retained for **5 years** post-termination.
3. **Customer Contractual Documents:** Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), and non-disclosure agreements must be preserved for **6 years** following contract expiration.
4. **Information Security Incident Logs:** SIEM alerts, access logs, and firewall event streams must be archived for **3 years**.

## 4. Regulatory Compliance Obligations

- **GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure):** Customer data subject deletion requests must be acknowledged within **48 hours** and fulfilled across primary databases and backup cold storage within **30 calendar days**.
- **CCPA / CPRA Disclosures:** Annual privacy metric reports must be published by **July 1** of each fiscal year.
- **Audit Mandates:** An independent third-party SOC 2 Type II audit must be conducted annually in **Q3**.

## 5. Exceptions, Violations, and Risk Mitigations

Any deviation from retention timelines requires written sign-off from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and General Counsel. Unapproved retention of Tier 4 PII beyond specified windows carries disciplinary actions up to contract termination.

### ITEM 1. BUSINESS

#### Overview & Strategic Product Architecture
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. In fiscal year 2024, the Company unveiled **Apple Intelligence**, a personal intelligence system integrated deeply into iOS 18, iPadOS 18, and macOS Sequoia, combining on-device generative models with Private Cloud Compute to maintain strict user privacy standards.

The Company’s fiscal year is the 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

#### Fiscal Year 2024 Financial Highlights
- **Total Net Sales:** $391.0 billion, an increase of $7.8 billion or 2% compared to $383.3 billion in fiscal year 2023.
- **Services Net Sales:** $96.2 billion, an increase of $11.0 billion or 13% compared to $85.2 billion in fiscal year 2023. Services gross margin reached an all-time high of 74.0%.
- **Products Net Sales:** $294.9 billion, compared to $298.1 billion in fiscal year 2023, reflecting a 1% contraction due to elongated smartphone replacement cycles in Greater China.
- **Operating Income:** $123.2 billion, an increase of 8% compared to $114.3 billion in fiscal year 2023.
- **Operating Margin:** 31.5%, expanding from 29.8% in fiscal year 2023.
- **Net Income:** $93.7 billion, compared to $97.0 billion in fiscal year 2023 (impacted by a one-time European Commission state aid tax charge of $10.2 billion).

### ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

#### 1. Geopolitical Exposures and Supply Chain Concentration in Greater China
A substantial portion of the Company's manufacturing, assembly, and customer revenue is concentrated in Greater China and adjacent regions. Trade restrictions, escalating tariffs, local regulatory restrictions on foreign technology usage, and economic headwinds in the Chinese consumer market directly affect hardware gross margins and unit sales volumes.

#### 2. Digital Markets Act (DMA) & Global Antitrust Regulatory Pressures
The European Union’s Digital Markets Act (DMA) designates Apple as a "gatekeeper" and mandates structural modifications to the iOS ecosystem, including allowing alternative app marketplaces, alternative payment processing mechanisms, and unbundled default browser engines. These regulatory shifts create operational complexity, increase security attack surfaces, and reduce Apple's App Store take-rate economics.

#### 3. On-Device AI Monetization & Silicon Supply Dependency
The commercial success of Apple Intelligence relies on custom Apple Silicon (A17 Pro, A18, M4 series chips) fabricated exclusively by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC). Any disruption to TSMC’s advanced lithography nodes or failure of consumer demand to generate an AI smartphone upgrade supercycle could impair financial growth.
